# Motors

Buyers want **the right car at a fair price without being misled**. Sellers want serious buyers. Dealers want leads.

## Arc (`motors.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | results grid of cars | "Find your next car" · "Every make, near you" |
| 2 | Filters | make / model / year / km / price filters | "Narrow it to the one" · "Search by budget" |
| 3 | Car details | DPV: photos, specs, price | "Every detail upfront" · "Know it before you see it" |
| 4 | Dealers & Verified | dealer page, Verified / Pro badge | "Buy from verified dealers" |
| 5 | Sell your car | post-a-car flow or valuation | "Sell your car the easy way" |
| 6 | Closer | feature wall: finance, comparison, new cars, alerts | |

## Do

- Real makes and models sold in the market, plausible year/km/price.
- Magnifier on the price, mileage or Verified badge.
- Car finance or comparison tools, if shipped, are a strong differentiator screen.

## Don't

- A private seller's number plate or face. Blur or choose dealer stock.
- "Best price guaranteed", "inspected" or "certified" unless the app has that programme, named as it is in the app.
- Finance rates without the conditions; treat finance screens with `fintech.md` rules.

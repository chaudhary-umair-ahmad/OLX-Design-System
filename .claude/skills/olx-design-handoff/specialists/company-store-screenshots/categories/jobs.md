# Jobs

Seekers want **relevant jobs and a quick way to apply**. Employers want **qualified applicants fast**.

## Arc (`jobs.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | job feed matched to a profile | "Find work near you" · "Your next job, today" |
| 2 | Search & alerts | filters, saved search, alert | "Jobs that fit you" · "Get alerts first" |
| 3 | Apply | one-tap apply / application sent | "Apply in one tap" |
| 4 | Profile / CV | profile or CV builder | "Your CV, ready on your phone" |
| 5 | Employers | post a job / candidate list (sample people) | "Hire faster" |
| 6 | Closer | categories or feature wall | |

## Don't

- Salaries that aren't in the listing; "guaranteed job", "hired in 24 hours".
- Real candidates' names, photos or CVs; real employers' logos without permission.
- Listings with age, gender, nationality or religion requirements in the capture.

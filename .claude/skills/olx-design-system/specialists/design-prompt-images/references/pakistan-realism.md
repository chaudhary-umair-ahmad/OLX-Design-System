# Pakistan realism: nouns that make an image local

Use 5–12 of these in the SETTING slot. Pick one city and stay in it.

## Streets and homes

- **Karachi:** apartment blocks with balconies and laundry, water tanks on roofs, sea haze, DHA / Clifton bungalows with boundary walls and gates, rickshaws, Suzuki pick-ups.
- **Lahore:** brick and plaster houses, Bahria Town / DHA phases with wide roads, roadside dhabas, motorbikes in rows, canal bank trees, winter smog in Dec–Jan.
- **Islamabad / Rawalpindi:** Margalla hills behind, sector markets (F-7, G-9), wide green belts, cleaner roads.
- Everywhere: overhead electric cables, a UPS or solar panel on the roof, a generator by the gate, a chowkidar's chair, plastic chairs, potted plants on a stair.
- Inside homes: tiled floors, sofa with a printed cover, a ceiling fan, an AC split unit, a dining table with a plastic cover, a showcase cabinet.

## Vehicles (generic, no badges)

Right-hand drive, traffic on the left. "A small white hatchback typical of Pakistani roads" (Alto / Cultus class),
"a silver compact sedan" (City / Corolla class), "a red 70cc motorbike" (CD 70 class), auto-rickshaws, Suzuki
Bolan vans. **Never** a make, model, badge or readable plate; plates are angled away or blank.

## People

- Everyday clothing: shalwar kameez for men and women, dupatta, jeans and kurta for younger people, waistcoats in winter.
- Women with and without dupatta over the head is realistic; don't make every woman veiled or none.
- Families of mixed ages. Skin tones across the real range; don't lighten.
- Never Indian-wedding styling, Gulf dress, or Western suburban looks as shorthand.

## Light and season

Harsh midday sun, dusty late-afternoon gold, monsoon grey (Jul–Sep) with wet roads, winter haze in Punjab.
Load-shedding means emergency lights and UPS-lit rooms at night are believable.

## Things that read wrong

Left-hand-drive cars; snow outside the northern areas; manicured Western lawns in the city; spotless empty roads in Karachi or Lahore; English-only shop signs (signs are mixed Urdu and English, and **no text should render anyway**: keep signs out of frame or blurred).

# Quick commerce

Groceries and essentials delivered in minutes. The promise is **speed**, and it is also where claims get
products into trouble.

## Arc (`quick-commerce.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | home with aisles and an ETA | "Groceries at your door, fast" · "Forgot milk? Sorted." |
| 2 | Aisles / range | categories: fresh, household, baby | "Fresh food to phone chargers" |
| 3 | Reorder | previous orders / one-tap reorder | "Your usual, in one tap" |
| 4 | Live tracking | rider on the map | "Watch it come to you" |
| 5 | Pay your way | wallet, card, cash | |
| 6 | Offers | first-order or loyalty offers (real terms) | |

## Do

- Delivery-time claims exactly as the business can support them, qualified: "in as little as 15 minutes in
  selected areas". The qualifier is visible on the same screen.
- Real products and real current prices from the app's catalogue.

## Don't

- A bare "10-minute delivery" headline.
- A rider's real face or name, a customer's real address on the tracking map (use a sample address).
- Offer amounts or codes that aren't live at release time; offers age badly in a store listing.

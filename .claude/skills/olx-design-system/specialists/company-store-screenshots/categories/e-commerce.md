# E-commerce

Shoppers want **range, a fair price, delivery they can trust and easy returns**.

## Arc (`e-commerce.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | home with real categories / deals | "Everything you shop for, in one app" |
| 2 | Browse | category or search results | "Find it in seconds" |
| 3 | Product detail | photos, price, reviews, delivery date | "Know when it arrives" |
| 4 | Checkout & pay | checkout with local payment options | "Pay your way: card, wallet or cash" |
| 5 | Delivery & returns | tracking, returns flow | "Easy returns" (only if the policy is) |
| 6 | Offers | deals / loyalty | |

## Do

- Product photos as they appear in the app (the brands' own packshots are fine there).
- Local payment methods the app supports (cash on delivery, wallets, instalments).

## Don't

- Strike-through "was" prices or "% off" that aren't real, current prices in the app.
- "Free delivery" without its condition if one exists ("over EGP 500").
- "Original" or "authentic" guarantees unless the app runs that programme.
- Brand logos as decoration on the screenshot frame.

# Fintech

Wallets, payments, BNPL and instalments, lending, savings and investing, insurance. People want **control and
safety for their money**. This is a **regulated** category: the deck needs legal review before export.

## Arc (`fintech.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | home: balance or key action (sample numbers) | outcome first: "Pay bills in seconds" · "Split it into 4" |
| 2 | Core action | send money / pay / apply | "Send money in two taps" |
| 3 | Control & insight | spending view, limits, statements | "See where it all goes" |
| 4 | Security | biometric login, card freeze, alerts | "Freeze your card in one tap" |
| 5 | Fees / pricing | fee screen or plan | only with the real fee: "No fee on transfers under X" |
| 6 | Licensed & trusted | **legal's licence line** | slot left for legal |

## Required before export

- **Licence / regulator line** where the market requires it (e.g. the central bank or financial regulator that
  licenses the product). Legal writes it; you leave a marked text slot: `[LICENCE LINE — from legal]`.
- **Representative example** next to any rate or instalment claim (APR, fees, total payable), as legal words it.
- Google Play's **Financial Services** policy (extra declarations, and limits for personal-loan apps) and
  Apple's guideline on financial apps: check with the product team that the listing matches the declarations.

## Don't

- "Guaranteed returns", "risk-free", "instant approval", "0%" without the conditions, "no hidden fees".
- Real card numbers, IBANs, balances, names or transactions. Sample numbers only; make the card number
  obviously fake (`•••• 4242`).
- Bank or card-network logos unless the partnership and its brand guidelines allow it.
- Crypto or investment performance charts with real-looking past returns.

# Rentals

Long-term homes, holiday homes, cars or equipment. Renters want **availability and the full price upfront**.
Owners want **reliable renters and payment**.

## Arc (`rentals.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | results for a real area and dates | "Rent it for a night or a year" · "Stay where you like" |
| 2 | Search | dates / area / budget | "Pick your dates, see what's free" |
| 3 | Listing & price | photos, price per night/month, what's included | "The full price, upfront" |
| 4 | Book or contact | booking flow or contact owner | "Book in a few taps" |
| 5 | Trust & payments | reviews, secure payment, deposit terms | "Pay safely in the app" |
| 6 | Owners | list your place / manage bookings | "List your place free" (only if it is free) |

## Don't

- A per-night price shown without taxes and fees if the app adds them at checkout.
- "Free cancellation" unless every listing on screen offers it.
- Real guests' reviews with names and faces; write sample reviews only for captures, and only if the app's
  capture tooling allows sample data.

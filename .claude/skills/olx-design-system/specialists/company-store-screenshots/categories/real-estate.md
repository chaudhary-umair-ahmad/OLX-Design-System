# Real estate

Buyers and renters want **real listings, real prices, the right area**. Agents and developers want leads.
It's the category where trust ("is this listing real?") matters most.

## Arc (`real-estate.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | search results or map with homes | "Find your next home" · "Homes in every area" |
| 2 | Search & map | map search, area / budget filters | "Search the map" · "Your budget, your area" |
| 3 | Listing detail | photos, price, size, amenities | "See it all before you visit" |
| 4 | Projects | new projects / off-plan, payment plans | "New homes, straight from developers" |
| 5 | Agents & Verified | verified listing / agent badge | "Verified listings, marked" |
| 6 | Tools | mortgage calculator, price trends, saved searches | feature wall |

## Do

- Market units and price formats: sqm / sqft / marla / kanal as the app shows them; "from" prices for projects.
- Magnifier on the Verified badge or the price per sqm.
- Separate decks for consumer apps and agent/agency apps (agent tools follow `saas.md`).

## Don't

- A developer's project, renders or logo without permission. A render must never look like a finished building.
- Price-trend or "price will rise" claims without the data source.
- Markets that require an advertising licence number on property ads (e.g. KSA): leave a marked slot for the
  number legal gives you. Never write one.
- Agents' real names, phone numbers or photos unless they agreed.

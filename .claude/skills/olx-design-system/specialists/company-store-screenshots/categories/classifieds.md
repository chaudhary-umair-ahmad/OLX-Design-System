# Classifieds / marketplace

People come for two jobs: **sell something fast** and **find something near them at a fair price**. Both sides
have the same fear: wasting time on, or being cheated by, a stranger.

## Arc (`classifieds.json`)

| # | Slot | Show in the capture | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | home feed with real categories | "Buy and sell anything" is the weak default. Better: "Sell it today. Not someday." · "Everything you need, nearby" |
| 2 | Sell | Post an Ad flow: photos step or the review page | "Post an ad in a minute" · "Snap. Price. Sold." |
| 3 | Search | results with filters applied (location, price) | "Only what's near you" · "Filter to the exact one" |
| 4 | Chat | a conversation with an offer (sample names) | "Chat before you meet" · "Make an offer in-app" |
| 5 | Trust | Verified badge, safety tips, report | "Verified sellers, marked" · "Meet safe. Pay safe." |
| 6 | Closer | category grid or feature wall | "Cars. Homes. Phones. Jobs." |

## Do

- Show categories that match the market (the brand profile lists them).
- Use a believable item and price in captures: a real model, a typical local price.
- Seller tools (Promote, Sell faster, Boost) make a strong screen for seller-focused releases.

## Don't

- "Largest marketplace", "millions of ads", "#1" without a source and date.
- Promise sales ("Sell in 24 hours") or safety ("100% safe").
- Show a real person's ad, face, phone number or number plate.

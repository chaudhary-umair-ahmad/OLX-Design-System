# SaaS / B2B

CRMs, agent and dealer tools, inventory and lead management, business dashboards. The buyer is often a
manager; the user is someone on the move. Sell **the job done**, not the feature list.

## Arc (`saas.json`)

| # | Slot | Show | Headline directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | the main working screen | "Every lead, in one place" · "Run your agency from your phone" |
| 2 | Core workflow | the action they repeat daily | "Reply to leads in seconds" |
| 3 | Dashboard / insight | KPIs with **sample** data | "Know what's working" |
| 4 | Collaboration | assign, comment, share | "Your whole team, in sync" |
| 5 | On the go | notifications, offline | "Never miss a lead" |
| 6 | Closer | integrations and extras | feature wall |

## Do

- iPad and Mac decks are often worth it here (`split-landscape` layout, see upstream's Mac notes).
- Dashboards with plausible, round-ish sample numbers; the same numbers across screens.
- Mention who it's for in the hero label ("FOR AGENTS", "FOR DEALERS").

## Don't

- Customer logos or integration logos without permission.
- ROI claims ("3× more leads") without a sourced study.
- Real client names, leads or phone numbers in a CRM capture.
